Battery lifetime modelling

Industry project with SAFT · KTH · Jun 2026 – present

Overview

Coupled electrochemical–thermal–ageing modelling of a SAFT cell in COMSOL. Operating variables include temperature, C-rate, state-of-charge window and depth of discharge.

The work connects cell-test design, calibration and automated parameter studies. Outputs include capacity fade, resistance and heating.
