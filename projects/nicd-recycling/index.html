Ni–Cd battery recycling

Industry project with SAFT · KTH · Jun 2026 – present

Overview

Hydrometallurgical separation of cadmium and recovery of nickel from spent Ni–Cd battery powder, in collaboration with SAFT.

XRD, SEM-EDS, ICP-OES and particle-size analysis support material characterisation. Leaching and separation studies examine acid concentration, pH, temperature, redox potential, solid-to-liquid ratio and time.
